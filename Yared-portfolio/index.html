— Selected Work

Projects

Two projects that show how I lead a team and how I build a prototype end to end.

Team Leader · 2024/2025

Hiwet AI — Disease Detection for Indigenous Crops

An AI mobile app that detects diseases in teff, sorghum, barley and cactus pear from a photo, working offline and with Tigrigna support. I planned a CNN built from scratch with TensorFlow, Keras and Google Colab, trained on real local images, and a school committee to train 100+ students. The project was a Zayed Sustainability Prize finalist and secured a USD 25,000 grant, and we presented at the award ceremony in Abu Dhabi, UAE.

Developer · 2025/2026

Hiwet Smart Poultry Management System

A prototype robot and Android app that detect poultry diseases such as Newcastle disease and coccidiosis from images and recommend actions. I trained a MobileNet-based model and exported it to ONNX for on-device use in an Android Studio app with Amharic and English support. The robot uses an Arduino Uno, an ESP32 Wi-Fi module and DC motors, streams a live camera feed, and monitors temperature and humidity to control a fan and lamps.
